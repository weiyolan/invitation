Yolan's 30th
Idanha-a-Nova · Portugal

Boom Festival is Portugal's legendary biennial gathering — born in 1997 and held every two years since on the banks of the Marechal Carmona Dam in Idanha-a-Nova, it's far more than a music event, blending psychedelic and psytrance sound with immersive art, sustainability innovation and a community-driven ethos, all run with zero commercial branding and famously free of corporate sponsors.

The next edition runs 18 to 25 July 2027, under a full moon, and marks 30 years of cosmic psychedelic dancing — landing in the very same year I turn 30, which makes it non-negotiable.

Come mentally prepared for a full week of camping under the blazing Alentejo sun (July temperatures can top 40°C), sunrise sets and communal ritual, so pack light, hydrate hard, and be ready to properly disconnect.

One catch: Boom has been selling out since 2014, historically within hours, and ticket sales open after summer 2026 — so if you're in, lock it in your calendar now, because this one only comes around every two years and this edition is the big one.
